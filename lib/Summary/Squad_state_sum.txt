File Name: squad_state.dart
Purpose: Central state management for squad data, synced with Firestore, using ChangeNotifier.
Key Components:
SquadState (class with ChangeNotifier): Manages squad spots, timers, members, statuses, and more.
Core Data: squadSpots, spotTimers, squadMembers, statuses, peacockTimers, peacockQueue, gameHistory, etc.
initState: Initializes auth, data, Firestore sync, and periodic timer.
_syncWithFirestore: Listens to Firestore updates.
updateFirestore: Syncs state to Firestore with interval or force.
Methods:
Spot: assignSpot, removeSpot, claimSpot, lockSpot.
Game: recordWin, recordLoss.
Peacock: startPeacockTimer, reupPeacock, claimPeacockDialog, managePeacock.
Ban: addBan, getBanCount, isBanned, getBanDuration.
Timer: updateSpotTimers, updatePeacockTimers, getPeacockTimerDisplay.
Typing: updateTypingStatus, getTypingUser.
Helpers: _assignNextFromQueue, _checkAchievements.
Provider Usage:
ChangeNotifier: Notifies listeners on state changes for UI updates.
Dependencies:
flutter/material.dart, dart:async, cloud_firestore, firebase_auth, audioplayers, shared_preferences.
Custom: notification_service.dart, rating_dialog.dart.
Notes:
Syncs with Firestore squad/state every 5 seconds or on force.
Manages 4 squad spots and peacock queue with 300s/3600s timers.
Tracks game history, streaks, ratings, bans, and achievements.
Plays audio for wins and achievements; sends notifications on wins.
Bans expire after 4 hours; thresholds at 5 (24h) and 7 (48h).
